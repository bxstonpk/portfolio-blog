HR requests — leave, document requests, onboarding tasks — were being tracked over email and spreadsheets for a company of over 400 employees. That doesn't scale.

## The Situation

Manual tracking meant slow turnaround, duplicated effort, and requests falling through the cracks. HR staff were spending most of their time on repetitive coordination instead of higher-value work.

## What I Built

A small internal web app that:

- Lets employees submit and track requests online
- Automatically routes requests to the right approver
- Sends notification emails at each step
- Gives HR a dashboard of everything in flight

## Stack

Python and Flask for the backend, with MySQL for storage. I kept the frontend intentionally simple — this was about removing friction, not building something flashy.

## Impact

Manual coordination work dropped significantly, and employees got visibility into their own requests for the first time instead of having to ask HR for status updates.
